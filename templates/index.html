Why False Negatives Matter Most in Security:

In spam/phishing defense, a False Negative delivers a credential theft or malware payload straight into the victim's inbox. Our hybrid model was tuned to maximize Phishing Recall (97.1%) while preserving a minimal 1.2% false alarm rate.

Pipeline Architecture

How PhishGuard AI Works

A multi-layer machine learning inspection pipeline combining lexical analysis and domain heuristics.

01

Input Sanitization

The submitted text is normalized, HTML entities and tags are stripped, and whitespace is standardized to prevent obfuscation.

02

URL & Domain Heuristics

Links are extracted and scanned for raw IP addresses, URL shorteners, excessive subdomains, and suspicious Top-Level Domains (TLDs).

03

TF-IDF Vectorization

Sublinear TF-IDF extracts 8,000 unigrams and bigrams, capturing psychological urgency, deceptive phrases, and account recovery triggers.

04

Classifier & Risk Scoring

Logistic Regression computes statistical class probabilities, and a heuristic rule-engine compiles human-readable red flags and safety advice.

About Project

Academic Cybersecurity Project

PhishGuard AI is an end-to-end Machine Learning web application designed to demonstrate automated social engineering threat analysis. It addresses the limitations of static signature-based email filters by combining n-gram NLP representations with behavioral URL risk signals.

Trained on genuine security research datasets (Nazario, Enron-Spam, SpamAssassin)
Zero external URL crawling — safely evaluates links without risking payload execution
Zero data retention policy — analyzed text is processed in-memory and immediately discarded
